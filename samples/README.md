# Sample boards

These boards were designed by the TrustPCB author and are released under the repository's MIT licence for testing and demonstration.

| Folder | Board |
|---|---|
| `nrf52840-node` | USB-powered nRF52840 sensor node with a printed meander antenna |
| `nrf52840-node-v2` | The same board after the engineer's hand edits for the free re-tune the review confirmed: L1 becomes a 0.47 pF series capacitor and C20 is fitted with 0.3 pF. Copper and geometry are unchanged, for version rechecks |
| `patch-2g5` | 2.5 GHz patch antenna on FR-4 |

Symbols, footprints and 3D model references that come from the KiCad libraries remain under the KiCad library licence, CC BY-SA 4.0 with the KiCad libraries exception, which does not bind designs that use them.

Both boards are test material. They are unverified designs and contain real faults, which is why they are useful here. Do not manufacture them as they are.
