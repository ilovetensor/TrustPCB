# Phase 6: Report

The server assembles the report from stored findings, evidence and results. Your last job is the summary.

If the review has a partial win (`improved_short_of_target`: a confirm gain of at least 3 dB in band that still misses the target), the summary leads with it: the match improved at confirm quality, the target is not met, and the best confirm-quality parts to try (refs and values). The server rejects a summary that does not. Accepted power is a mismatch figure; never call it efficiency or radiated power.

In a recheck, the summary opens with what changed since the previous version (`lead_the_summary_with` in the answer of `advance_phase`): the as-built worst S11 across the band and the accepted power in both versions, whether the result is consistent with the earlier prediction (the same solver run recalculated with the new parts, not an independent result, and never called "measured"), and whether the target is met. The server rejects a summary that does not open with it. Then come the partial win (if any) and the findings.

Call `submit_summary` once with a plain-language summary of what you found (what matters most first, what you tested, what you could not test) and up to five top actions for the engineer. Every number with a unit must match the evidence and the row its sentence cites, every paragraph that quotes S11 or accepted power must say simulated, counts and ranges come from the evidence statistics, nothing may contradict the confirm `verdict`, nothing may say the board was changed, and any finding code you mention must exist. If the summary is rejected, fix the named problem and try again; after three rejections the server writes the summary itself.

After the summary is accepted, stop.
