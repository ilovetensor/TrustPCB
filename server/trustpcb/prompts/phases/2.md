# Phase 2: Understand the board

Goal: know what this board is and what matters before judging anything. You have {phase2_calls} tool calls here. Finish with `submit_board_brief`, then `advance_phase`.

Do this:
1. `get_board_summary` for parts, nets, layers, stackup, the engineer's goal, the assumptions and how many analyzer results exist.
2. Look at the board with `view_board` (top first). Close-up looks you take now do not count toward the phase 3 requirement, so keep the detailed looks for phase 3. Use `read_schematic` to see what the important parts and nets are. Use `read_datasheet` only for a part whose role you cannot tell.
3. Work out: what the board does, which parts are the radio, power, clock, interface and sensitive parts, where the antenna and its feed are (or that there is none), and what could go wrong given the goal.

Submit the brief with the purpose in plain words, a role for each important part (use short roles such as radio, antenna_feed, matching, power_input, regulator, decoupling, crystal, usb, sensor, connector), the risks you want to check in order of worry, and your priorities. Name only parts and targets that exist.

If the engineer gave a focus, put it first in your priorities. Do not ask the engineer questions: say what you assumed in the brief.
