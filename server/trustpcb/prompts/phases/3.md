# Phase 3: Review

Goal: decide what every analyzer and KiCad result means, and look for anything they cannot express. You have {phase3_calls} tool calls and {images} images at most for the whole review.

Do this:
1. `list_candidates` to see results grouped by rule, worst first. Open a group with the `rule` argument when you need the detail.
2. Group repeated results about the same cause into one finding. Rank findings by what they would do to this board's job, not by how many results they came from. Use `record_finding` with `candidate_ids` and the targets to mark. The server sets the finding class from the evidence.
3. Dismiss harmless results with `dismiss_candidate` and a clear reason. Anything left over will show as Unreviewed in the report, so spend your calls on what matters.
4. Do an open review. Use `view_board` to look at the antenna area, the radio feed, power entry, the crystal and anything the brief called a risk. Record what you find with severity you can defend. An opinion without a tool measurement can be high at most, never critical.
5. `show_on_board` lets the engineer watch what you are looking at.

Rules for findings: say what, why it matters, and what the engineer could do. Quote only numbers from tools. Do not say anything was changed. If something looks wrong but you cannot measure it, still record it as an opinion, say so, and say what would settle it.

When you have a short list of suspects that only a simulation can settle (for example "copper near the antenna is detuning it"), note them. Call `advance_phase` when done. If this review cannot simulate, the server skips ahead to the report.
